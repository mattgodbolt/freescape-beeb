# Across an area's edge. The walk stops at the area's extent ($88DF-$88E4,
# cells 0-127 across); there the margin's test ($BCCB) finds the edge's
# rectangle (ids 51-54), whose script is GOTO area, 0. Entrance 0 isn't
# found, so $9D1C wraps the camera: x cell 126 to 1, 0 to 125 (else z),
# keeping y and the angles.

# Area 5's entrance 6 is on a road at x cell 125, looking along +z; turn to
# +x and walk off the x=127 edge into area 6.
goto 5 6
W x6
expect Yaw 18
O x2
expect AreaId 6
expect PlayerX 96
O
# Back the other way: area 6's x=0 edge has a kerb a cell high (cube 21)
# to climb first, then rectangle 54 goes back to area 5, at x cell 125.
U
K
O x3
expect AreaId 5
expect PlayerX 8032
O x2

# The z edges: area 5's z=0 rectangle (52) goes to area 11, and area 11's
# z=127 one back.
goto 5 7
U
O x2
expect AreaId 11
expect PlayerZ 8032
O
U
O x3
expect AreaId 5

# At a slant into the edge, by small steps.
goto 5 6
W x5
X x4
expect Step 10
O x40
expect AreaId 6

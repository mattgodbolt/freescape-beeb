# Through doorways into other areas. A doorway is a rectangle on a wall's
# face whose collided script is GOTO area, entrance: the move into it sets
# Collided, and the events run the script, which puts the camera at the
# entrance ($9D1C) with the entrance's angles.

# Area 1's rectangle 12 (x cell 126, z cells 72-74) goes to area 2's
# entrance 4. The wall (cube 4) behind it is hit first and remembered; the
# rectangle, a flat shape, wins at once.
at 7840 160 4704
angles 0 18 0
O
expect Collided 12
expect AreaId 2
expect EntranceId 4
expect PlayerX 224
expect Yaw 18
O x2
# Back through area 2's rectangle 3 (x cell 1, z cells 63-65) to area 1's
# entrance 27.
U
O x3
expect AreaId 1
expect EntranceId 27
O x3

# A doorway reached at a slant, and by small steps, so the margin's test
# finds it ($BCCB) before a step's crossing does.
at 7840 160 4650
angles 0 15 0
X x3
expect Step 25
O x10
expect AreaId 2
S x3

# Area 1's rectangle 20 (x cells 66-68, z cell 36) goes to area 19, an
# interior at scale 4: the step and the probe's height scale with it.
goto 1 19

angles 0 36 0
O x2
expect AreaId 19
expect AreaScale 4
expect StepUnits 1000
expect BodyHeight 8

R
